## Filming notes

- Shoot in the **early evening with lamps on**. That's what makes the photos look warm. Terrace and beach at **dusk**.
- Hold the phone **vertical**, walk slowly, and use the 0.5x lens for rooms. Turn on stabilisation (Action mode on iPhone).
- Keep on-screen text **away from the top and bottom fifth** of the frame and off the right edge. Instagram's buttons cover those areas.
- Add **burned-in captions** (Instagram's Captions sticker, or CapCut). Most people start watching muted.
- No sea view from the house, so the sea only appears in the beach shot, after you've walked out the gate.
